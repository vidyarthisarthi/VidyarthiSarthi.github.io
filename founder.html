The Story

Rashid Ali Khan began his journey not in a startup incubator or an IIT lab, but in a physical classroom in Lucknow. For approximately 18 years, he taught Physics at Genius Academy, Rajendra Nagar — observing thousands of students struggle, not because they lacked intelligence, but because the teaching system could not find exactly where their understanding broke down.

That insight became an obsession: what if a system could identify the precise moment a student's thinking diverged from correct reasoning, and ask exactly the right question to bring it back on track? Not give the answer — ask the right question. This is the Socratic constraint at the heart of VidyarthiSarthi.ai.

Self-taught in Flutter, LLM engineering, cloud architecture, and patent law, Rashid transitioned from educator to inventor — filing 12 patent families with the Indian Patent Office in a 38-day sprint between June and July 2026, covering 144 claims and 72 figures. He is the sole inventor on all twelve families.
